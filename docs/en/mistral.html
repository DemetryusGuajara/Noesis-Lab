Mistral AI (La Plateforme)

Endpoint locked in the app:
https://api.mistral.ai/v1

1. Create your key

  1. Go to console.mistral.ai/api-keys and sign in.
  2. Click Create new key (optionally restrict it) and copy the value.

Free tier: Mistral offers free rate-limited access on several models; heavier use is pay-as-you-go. Check official pricing and the model list.

2. Use it in Noesis Lab

  1. Settings → Mistral AI Studio → + Add key → paste → Save, Test & Filter Models.
  2. Pick a responding model chip (★ = active).
